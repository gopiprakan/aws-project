# 🌍 Sahaaya AI – AI Platform for Community Access to Public Services

## 🚀 Overview
Sahaaya AI is an AI-powered digital platform designed to help citizens easily access government services, welfare schemes, and public information. It provides chatbot assistance, voice support, and document processing to reduce complexity and improve digital inclusion.

This project is developed as part of a Hackathon under the theme **AI for Communities, Access & Public Impact**.

---

## 🎯 Problem Statement
Many citizens, especially in rural and underserved communities, face difficulties in understanding and accessing government services due to language barriers, lack of awareness, and complex procedures.

Sahaaya AI aims to bridge this gap by offering a simple, intelligent, and multilingual digital assistant.

---

## 💡 Solution
Sahaaya AI uses Artificial Intelligence to:
- Answer user queries about public services
- Recommend eligible government schemes
- Read and process official documents
- Provide voice-based and multilingual support
- Track application status

The platform ensures easy, fast, and transparent access to public resources.
